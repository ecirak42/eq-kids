Why parents enroll

School teaches math. Sports teach teamwork. Who teaches feelings?

Most kids never get direct practice with the skills that shape their whole life: calming down after disappointment, joining a group, repairing a conflict, and trying again after mistakes. EQ Kids Club is a weekly space built just for that.

3

Confidence gets practiced

Common questions

What parents usually ask.

Is this therapy?

No. EQ Kids Club is an educational enrichment program, much like a sports team for emotional skills. Kids practice tools through games and activities. It is not counseling and not a substitute for it.

My kid is shy / my kid is high-energy. Will this work for them?

Both, genuinely. Sessions mix movement and quiet activities, and the small group size means Hanako can meet each child where they are. Shy kids are never put on the spot; high-energy kids get plenty of room to move.

What happens after I apply?

We review your application and reach out within one day to talk through schedule, group fit, and any questions. If it feels like a good match, we explain the next step and help you choose the right weekly group.

Does applying commit me to anything?

No. Applying is free and simply starts the conversation. It helps us understand your child, answer your questions, and make sure the group would be a good fit before you make a decision.

Why an application instead of just signing up?

Because groups are capped at 12 and grouped by age, we place kids carefully rather than first-come-first-served. The short application helps us build groups where every kid thrives.

How long is each class?

Classes are planned as two-hour weekly sessions. We'll share the confirmed day and time before enrollment begins.

Where exactly is The Monday Club?

The Monday Club is a historic community space near downtown Webster Groves, with easy parking and pickup. We'll send full directions and drop-off details when we confirm your spot.
