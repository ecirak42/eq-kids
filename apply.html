Why parents enroll

School teaches math. Sports teach teamwork. Who teaches feelings?

Most kids never get direct practice with the skills that shape their whole life: naming emotions, calming down, working through friendship problems, and recovering from mistakes. EQ Kids Club is a weekly space built just for that.

Common questions

What parents usually ask.

Is this therapy?

No. EQ Kids Club is an educational enrichment program — like a sports team for emotional skills. Kids practice tools through games and activities. It is not counseling and not a substitute for it.

My kid is shy / my kid is high-energy. Will this work for them?

Both, genuinely. Sessions mix movement and quiet activities, and the small group size means Hanako can meet each child where they are. Shy kids are never put on the spot; high-energy kids get plenty of room to move.

What happens after I apply?

We review your application and reach out within one day to talk through schedule, group fit, and any questions. Then your child comes to their first Saturday free. If they love it (they usually do), you enroll at the founding rate. If not, you owe nothing.

Is the first class really free? What's the catch?

Really free, no catch. EQ is something you want to see before you buy — and we'd rather earn your enrollment than talk you into it. Your child joins a real session, and you decide afterward.

Why an application instead of just signing up?

Because groups are capped at 12 and grouped by age, we place kids carefully rather than first-come-first-served. The short application helps us build groups where every kid thrives.

It's Saturday lunchtime — should my kid eat first?

Yes, please send them with lunch in their belly. Sessions run 12–2pm and are active, so a fed kid is a happy kid.

Where exactly is The Monday Club?

The Monday Club is a historic community space near downtown Webster Groves, with easy parking and pickup. We'll send full directions and drop-off details when we confirm your spot.
