The program

A steady weekly space to practice what kids use every day.

Each EQ Kids session blends movement, stories, discussion, hands-on activities, and reflection. Kids learn to recognize what is happening inside them, communicate with more clarity, and recover when a moment gets hard.

The format is built for elementary-aged students: active, warm, concrete, and never lecture-heavy.
